The hunt, from the cat's eyes

01 / 10

Hour six.

It's the sixth hour of an empty afternoon. The sun has crawled halfway across the floor, and nothing in this house has moved in hours.

Your world sits a hand's width off the ground. You've been waiting all day for something, anything, to happen.

02 / 10

Something moved.

Then, at the edge of the rug, a feather twitches.

Your pupils swell until the whole room goes soft and only that one point stays sharp.

03 / 10

Freeze.

It stops dead. So do you.

Every muscle loads like a spring, because anything that holds this still is hiding something.

04 / 10

Dart.

It streaks across the sunlight and vanishes behind the cushion. A long pause. Then the tip of it shows again, testing the air.

Real prey never runs in circles. It runs, it hides, and it checks whether you're still watching. So does this.

05 / 10

Pounce.

Now.

Got it. Both paws, every claw, the whole long afternoon spent in a single leap. Every hunt deserves an ending you can catch.

06 / 10

Who's moving the toy?

Meet the Prekko Pawbot.

It runs play sessions on its own and stops when your cat loses interest.

It doesn't play back a loop. Every move is chosen live, for your cat. Working prototype shown; final design may differ.

07 / 10

Three layers of AI.

Three AI systems, working as one.

  1. 01SeeVision AI tracks your cat in real time: where they are, how fast they move, where they look, how low they crouch and how interested they are.
  2. 02ChooseA strategy layer reads your cat's mood and picks from 12 prey patterns, playing the moves your cat chases.
  3. 03MoveA motion layer fine-tunes that pattern with 8 parameters, adjusted 30 times a second.

12 prey patterns. 8 parameters each. 30 updates a second.

Endless variations. No preset loops.

It learns your cat.

Every session teaches it something: which moves make your cat crouch and chase, and which ones they walk away from.

Overnight, it trains on the day's play and comes back sharper.

It learns how your cat plays and gets better at playing with your cat over time.
